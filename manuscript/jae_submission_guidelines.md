# Journal of Animal Ecology — Submission & Formatting Guidelines

*Source: [JAE Author Guidelines](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/13652656/author-guidelines) (accessed 2026-03-20). For revised manuscript guidelines, see the separate revisions page.*

---

## 1. Article Types & Word Limits

| Type | Word limit | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **Research Article** | **8,500 words** | Includes title page, abstract, main text, references, tables, and figure legends |
| **Review** | 8,500 words | Must provide genuine advances, not just literature surveys |
| **Long-term Studies** | 8,500 words | Synthesis of established study systems; primarily published findings |
| **Research Methods Guide** | 7,000 words | Practical instructional papers |
| **Concept** | 3,000 words | Short essay on emerging ideas; up to 4 tables/figures |
| **Forum** | Short | Responses to published material; no new data |

**Word count includes**: title page, abstract, main text, references, tables, and figure legends.
**Word count excludes**: Supporting Information only.
**If over the limit**: provide justification in the cover letter.

---

## 2. Submission Format (Initial)

Initial submissions are simplified. Requirements:

- [ ] Single column, **double line-spaced**
- [ ] Within the word count (8,500 for Research Articles)
- [ ] **Line and page numbers** (continuous throughout)
- [ ] Clearly defined structure: Title page → Abstract → Introduction → Materials and Methods → Results → Discussion → References
- [ ] Figures and tables **can be embedded** within the text where referenced, with legends
- [ ] Statement of where you intend to archive your data
- [ ] **Any file type** accepted for initial submission

**Manuscripts may be returned if these requirements are not met.**

---

## 3. Double-Anonymised Review

JAE uses **double-anonymised** peer review:

- **Title page must be uploaded as a SEPARATE file** from the main manuscript.
- **Remove all obviously identifiable references to authors** from the main text and Supporting Information.
- Instead of "in our previous study," write "in a previous study by [ref] et al."
- Typically reviewed by **two independent experts**.

---

## 4. Title Page (Separate File)

The title page must be a **separate uploaded file** containing:

1. **Title**: Concise and informative. Do not include taxonomic authorities.
2. **Author list**: All authors' names with institutional affiliations (names + addresses).
3. **Corresponding author**: Name, address, and email.
4. **Acknowledgements** (optional): Collaborators, research assistants (who don't meet authorship criteria), funding sources, permit numbers, recognition of nature reserves or organisations.
5. **Conflict of Interest** (required): Disclose any potential conflicts (patent/stock ownership, employment by private company, advisory board membership, consultancy, speaker's fees). If none: "The authors declare no conflict of interest."
6. **Author Contributions** (required for multi-author papers): Must mention each author by name. CRediT taxonomy welcomed. Example format:
   > [Author 1] and [Author 3] conceived the ideas and designed methodology; [Author 1] and [Author 2] collected the data; [Author 1] and [Author 3] analysed the data; [Author 1] and [Author 2] led the writing of the manuscript. All authors contributed critically to the drafts and gave final approval for publication.

   Note: Acquiring funding, providing facilities, or supervising without additional contribution is **not sufficient** for authorship — list in Acknowledgements instead.

7. **Statement on Inclusion** (optional in published article, **required during submission**): Disclose actions taken to collaborate with and provide opportunities for stakeholders in the study region. See guidelines for example statements.

8. **Data Availability Statement**: List database, accession numbers, or DOIs for all publicly archived data. Example:
   > "Data available from the Dryad Digital Repository: https://doi.org/10.1111/1365-2656.12537 (Kiere & Drummond 2016)"

   When a DOI is available, include the full data citation in the reference list as well.

## 5. Main Manuscript (Anonymised)

### 5.1 Abstract
- **Maximum 350 words**.
- List main results and conclusions using simple, factual statements.
- Written in English.
- **Second abstract in native language or local language encouraged** — published online only (not in PDF), not copyedited.

### 5.2 Keywords
- Alphabetical order, **not exceeding eight words or short phrases**.
- Do not repeat words from the title.

### 5.3 Introduction
- State the reason for the work, hypotheses under consideration, and essential background.

### 5.4 Materials and Methods
- Sufficient detail for reproducibility.
- Include **ethical approval details** and permit numbers here.

### 5.5 Results
- State results; draw attention to important details in tables and figures.

### 5.6 Discussion
- Significance of results; context with other work.

### 5.7 Conclusions (optional)

### 5.8 References
- **In-text citations**: Author-date method — `(Jones, 1998)`.
- **Reference list**: Alphabetical by surname at end of paper.
- **DOI required** for all references where available.
- You will **not** be asked to reformat references during submission or peer review.
- See §7 below for format examples.

### 5.9 Data Sources (where appropriate)
- For submissions using data from multiple published sources (e.g., meta-analyses).
- Cite in Materials and Methods or in a separate "Data sources" section after References.
- Ensures proper citation credit for source authors.

---

## 6. Supporting Information (Optional)

- Published in the online version only.
- **Not copyedited** — authors are responsible for quality.
- BES **does not allow data sets** as Supporting Information — archive data in a public repository instead (Dryad, Zenodo, etc.).
- Reference in text as: Fig. S1, Table S1, Appendix S1.

---

## 7. References Format

### 7.1 In-text citations
- Author-date: `(Jones, 1998)`, `(Smith & Jones, 2005)`, `(Smith et al., 2010)`.
- Two authors: both names. Three or more: first author et al.
- Multiple citations: chronological, semicolons: `(Jones, 1998; Smith et al., 2010)`.
- Same author, same year: `(Jones, 1998a, 1998b)`.

### 7.2 Reference list
- Alphabetical by first author surname.
- **DOI required** for all references where available.
- All authors listed (no "et al." in reference list).

### 7.3 Examples (from JAE sample references)

**Journal article:**
> Stier, A.C., & Osenberg, C.W. (2024). Title of article. *Journal of Animal Ecology*, 93, 100–115. https://doi.org/10.xxxx

**Book:**
> Crawley, M.J. (2013). *The R Book*, 2nd ed. John Wiley & Sons, Chichester.

**Book chapter:**
> Hixon, M.A. (2015). Chapter title. In C. Mora (Ed.), *Ecology of Fishes on Coral Reefs* (pp. 41–52). Cambridge University Press.

## 8. Figures

- Cite in numerical order (Fig. 1, Fig. 2).
- **Can be embedded** in the text for initial submission (with legends).
- Multi-panel: label (a), (b), (c) consistently.
- All text legible at final printed size.
- High-resolution files required at revision/acceptance:
  - ≥300 dpi for colour/greyscale
  - ≥600 dpi for line art
  - Preferred: TIFF, EPS, PDF
- Colour is **free** (no charge) online and in print.
- Use **colourblind-friendly palettes**.

### Figure sizes
| Width | mm | inches |
|-------|-----|--------|
| Single column | 80 | 3.15 |
| 1.5 columns | 120 | 4.72 |
| Full width | 169 | 6.65 |
| Max height | 234 | 9.21 |

---

## 9. Tables

- Number consecutively (Table 1, Table 2).
- Can be embedded in text for initial submission.
- Legend/title above the table.
- Horizontal rules only (no vertical lines).
- Footnotes: superscript lowercase letters.

---

## 10. Species Names

- **First mention**: Full binomial in italics. Authorities are **not** required in the title.
- Authorities for taxonomic names should appear at first mention in the text (Methods or Introduction).
- Subsequent mentions: abbreviated genus (*P. meandrina*).
- Higher taxa capitalised, not italicised: Trapeziidae, Decapoda.

---

## 11. Spelling & Style

- **British English** (BES is a UK society): colour, favour, neighbouring, colonisation, defence, analyse, centre, behaviour, programme.
- **This manuscript uses American English** (color, favor, neighboring, colonization, defense, analyze, center, behavior, program). JAE accepts consistent spelling in either British or American convention; the requirement is internal consistency throughout the manuscript.
- Double line-spaced throughout.
- Continuous line numbers.
- 12-point standard font.

---

## 12. Cover Letter

- **Optional** for JAE.
- Must be **anonymous** (double-anonymised review).
- ≤500 words.
- Use to communicate: reasons for opposed reviewers, data archiving waiver requests, justification for exceeding word count, or a short explanation of novelty.
- Editors assess submissions based on manuscript content, not cover letters.

## 13. Submission System

- Submit via **ScholarOne Manuscripts**: https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/jae-besjournals
- Editorial Office: admin@journalofanimalecology.org

### Submission confirmations
Authors confirm that:
- Work has not been published or accepted elsewhere.
- All authors have read and approved the submission.
- All persons entitled to authorship are included.
- Work is original with proper acknowledgements.
- Work conforms to legal requirements (conservation, welfare).

---

## 14. Editorial Process

### Decision types
| Decision | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Reject** | Not acceptable; resubmission not considered |
| **Reject and resubmit** | Rejected but may submit a substantially revised new version |
| **Major revision** | Major changes needed; goes back through review; no guarantee |
| **Minor revision** | Minor changes; further review occasionally required; no guarantee |

### Appeals
- Submit within **1 month** of decision.
- 1-page document with general justification (not point-by-point rebuttal).
- Send to Editorial Office.

### Transfers
- Rejected manuscripts may be offered transfer to another BES journal with reviews.
