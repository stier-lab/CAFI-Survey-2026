# Manuscript: CAFI Survey Analysis

**Title**: Sublinear scaling of coral-associated fauna reveals propagule limitation in established reef communities

**Authors**: Adrian C. Stier et al.

**Study**: 114 *Pocillopora* coral colonies, 3 reef sites, Mo'orea, French Polynesia (Summer 2019)

---

## Core Research Questions & Results

| Q | Question | Key Finding | Figure |
|---|----------|-------------|--------|
| Q1 | How does CAFI scale with coral size? | Sublinear: abundance β=0.52, richness z=0.34 (Propagule Redirection); rarefied richness ns (p=0.64, passive sampling); density dilution | Fig 2, Fig 3 |
| Q2 | What structures CAFI composition? | Site pools + continuous size gradient (db-RDA: 7.8%, p=0.001, robust to rarefaction); not nested (NODF p=0.28); categorical size-divergence is abundance artifact | Fig 4 |
| Q3 | Does CAFI predict coral condition? | Species richness predicts condition (BEF framework, Hochberg p=0.036); variance partitioning: 29% unique to richness, <1% to abundance; rarefied richness ns but ambiguous | Fig 5 |
| Q4 | Does neighborhood density affect CAFI? | n_neighbors NOT significant for abundance or condition | S7 |

## Analytical Notes

- **Log base**: All models use natural log (`log()` in R)
- **Volume formula**: V = (4/3) * pi * (L/2) * (W/2) * (H/2) (ellipsoid approximation)
- **Multiple testing**: Three-tier for feedbacks (Hochberg FWER for a priori BEF k=2; BH-FDR for exploratory k=4); FDR within categories for scaling and co-occurrence
- **Site effects**: Fixed-effect site throughout (k=3 insufficient for random intercepts; Bolker et al. 2009)
- **Position correction**: Physiology traits regressed on stump_length + nubbin_length before PCA
- **Bootstrap**: 1,000 site-stratified iterations for scaling CIs
- **Co-occurrence**: Volume-weighted null model (logistic GLM predicted probs, Bernoulli draws, 10,000 iterations, SES + FDR)
- **CI types**: Fig 2 curves use profile likelihood CI; species forest plot uses bootstrap BCa CI
