### Issues Found

1. **[CROSS-01]** severity: major
   - Problem: "Species stacking" is introduced and defined in the Introduction ("a process termed 'species stacking'"; "the species-stacking constraint") and used in the Discussion but is entirely absent from the Methods and Results. The Results describe species-stacking-consistent patterns (turnover, nestedness rejection) but never invoke the term. A reader looking for where the species-stacking prediction was tested won't find it in Results — it appears only as an interpretive gloss in the Discussion.
   - Location: Intro (definition), Methods (absent), Results (absent), Discussion (invoked as established)
   - Proposed fix: Add one sentence to the Q2 Results paragraph reporting nestedness: "This pattern is consistent with the species-stacking constraint (Stier et al. 2012): intraspecific density ceilings force diversification with increasing colony size rather than accumulation of additional individuals of species already present." Also add a brief Methods note under Q2 framing the nestedness test as the species-stacking test.
   - Ripple risk: Results Q2 paragraph on nestedness; Methods Q2 last paragraph on NODF.

2. **[CROSS-02]** severity: major
   - Problem: The BEF acronym is introduced in the Methods Q3 subsection header ("Q3: CAFI diversity and coral condition (BEF framework)") but not in the Introduction. The Introduction paragraph introducing BEF (para 5) reads: "Biodiversity-ecosystem function (BEF) theory predicts..." — this actually does define it. However, the Q3 hypothesis in the Introduction ("Does CAFI species richness predict coral condition independently of abundance?") does not explicitly link back to "BEF" or "complementarity" in the hypothesis statement itself — only in the prediction sentence. The Results Q3 header reads "CAFI species richness is associated with coral condition" without BEF. The Discussion subsection is titled "Composition, co-occurrence, and the BEF question" — mixing three topics that were separate in Methods and Results. Q2 composition results and Q3 BEF results share a Discussion subsection, breaking the parallel Q1/Q2/Q3 structure that Methods and Results maintain.
   - Location: Intro (BEF defined but not labeled in Q3 prediction), Methods (Q3 = BEF), Results (Q3 header lacks BEF), Discussion (Q2+Q3 merged into one subsection)
   - Proposed fix: (a) Add "(BEF)" to the Results Q3 header: "Q3: CAFI species richness is associated with coral condition (BEF framework)". (b) Split the Discussion subsection "Composition, co-occurrence, and the BEF question" into two paragraphs with clear Q2 and Q3 anchors, or retitle it to signal the merged scope intentionally.
   - Ripple risk: Discussion subsection structure; Results Q3 header.

3. **[CROSS-03]** severity: major
   - Problem: Ambiguous figure reference for the composition divergence (betadisper) result. In the Results Q2 section: "Compositional divergence among size classes was not significant after controlling for abundance through rarefaction (betadisper: p = 0.61; Fig. S3)." But in the CLAUDE.md figure inventory, Fig. S3 = "Composition divergence by size" (from 02_community_analysis.R) and Fig. S5 = "Neighborhood null results." The Methods text under Q2 correctly ties composition divergence to Fig. S3. However, the Results section later (Q3 closing paragraph) reads "In the neighborhood/landscape models, coral volume, neighborhood density, and site did not predict condition (all p > 0.30; Fig. S5)." The figure numbers appear internally consistent within the manuscript, but the Discussion citation "Fig. S10A and S10C" appears in the BEF discussion without having been cited first in the Results body — these are orphaned Discussion citations.
   - Location: Results Q3 final paragraph; Discussion BEF paragraph
   - Proposed fix: Ensure Fig. S10A and S10C are cited first in the Results where variance partitioning and path model results are reported (they appear to be — "Fig. S10A" is cited in Results variance partitioning paragraph and "Fig. S10C" in the path model paragraph). Verify these citations exist in Results before the Discussion cites them again.
   - Ripple risk: Figures S10A, S10C references in Discussion.

4. **[CROSS-04]** severity: major
   - Problem: The Discussion's BEF paragraph resolves the rarefied richness ambiguity asymmetrically. The Results correctly presents the rarefied richness null as interpretively ambiguous ("We address the interpretive ambiguity of this test in the Discussion"). The Discussion then presents four reasons "several lines of evidence favour complementarity over a pure abundance pathway" — but does not give equal weight to the alternative interpretation (pure abundance artifact). The Abstract reports the finding as "consistent with biodiversity–ecosystem function theory, though the strong richness–abundance correlation (r = 0.84) and modest effect size prevent definitive causal attribution." The Discussion is less balanced than the Abstract on this point.
   - Location: Results Q3 (balanced), Abstract (balanced), Discussion BEF paragraph (asymmetrically favors complementarity)
   - Proposed fix: After the four-point case for complementarity, add a sentence explicitly acknowledging the equally viable alternative: "An equally parsimonious interpretation is that richness is simply a proxy for total occupancy, and the condition signal is driven entirely by the abundance pathway — a distinction our observational design cannot resolve." This mirrors the Abstract's balanced framing.
   - Ripple risk: Discussion BEF paragraph only; no cascading edits needed.

5. **[CROSS-05]** severity: minor
   - Problem: The "cross-study scaling concordance" block in Results ("Supporting results") is the only major result block that has no corresponding Discussion paragraph. The Discussion briefly mentions the temporal shift (colonization experiment vs. established community) in the sublinear scaling section, but the species-specific concordance table (Table S8, seven species) is never discussed. This is a dangling result.
   - Location: Results "Supporting results — Cross-study scaling concordance"; Discussion "Sublinear scaling and species stacking" paragraph
   - Proposed fix: Add 1–2 sentences to the Discussion's sublinear scaling section that connect the species-specific concordance result to the mechanistic argument about territorial species driving the temporal shift.
   - Ripple risk: Discussion sublinear scaling paragraph only.

6. **[CROSS-06]** severity: minor
   - Problem: The neighborhood null result appears twice (Results "Supporting results — Neighborhood effects"; Q3 closing sentence: "In the neighborhood/landscape models, coral volume, neighborhood density, and site did not predict condition") without connecting to the paper's central causal chain. The Introduction never poses a neighborhood question — Q1 is scaling with colony size, not neighborhood density — so the neighborhood results have no Introduction anchor. They appear as orphaned supplementary results. The Discussion never mentions neighborhood density at all.
   - Location: Results neighborhood paragraph; Discussion (absent)
   - Proposed fix: Either (a) add one sentence in the Discussion connecting the neighborhood null to the main narrative ("The absence of neighborhood effects suggests that local CAFI recruitment is governed by individual colony architecture rather than patch-level reef context, reinforcing the primacy of within-colony processes in this system"), or (b) remove the neighborhood results from the main Results section and relegate entirely to Supplement, noting only "Neighborhood effects are reported in the Supplement."
   - Ripple risk: Results Q3 final sentence (remove or reframe); Discussion closing or sublinear section.

7. **[CROSS-07]** severity: minor
   - Problem: The Introduction final paragraph frames the three questions as "successive links in a hypothesized causal chain from habitat quantity through community assembly to habitat condition." The Discussion opening paragraph reprises this as a "feedback loop." "Causal chain" (Intro) and "feedback loop" (Discussion) are not synonymous — a chain is unidirectional, a loop implies bidirectionality. The Results do test the reverse direction (condition → CAFI) and find no significant effect, but the shift from "chain" to "loop" terminology is not explained and slightly overstates what was tested.
   - Location: Intro final paragraph ("causal chain"), Discussion opening ("feedback loop")
   - Proposed fix: Either harmonize the terminology (use "feedback loop" in both places and add a brief Intro sentence acknowledging the loop framing) or use "causal chain" in the Discussion opening and reserve "loop" for the heatwave/implications section where the bidirectional framing is appropriate. The latter requires only a one-word change in the Discussion opening sentence.
   - Ripple risk: Intro final paragraph; Discussion opening sentence. Abstract also says "feedback loop" — change there if harmonizing to "chain."

8. **[CROSS-08]** severity: minor
   - Problem: The Methods statistical overview paragraph states "The two a priori BEF predictors (richness and total abundance) were corrected with Hochberg's step-up procedure (k = 2)." The Results Q3 reports p-values for richness (p = 0.018) and total abundance (p = 0.048) as if they are uncorrected, stating only "Species richness positively predicted coral condition." There is no mention of the Hochberg correction being applied to these values in the Results text, leaving the reader uncertain whether the reported p-values are raw or corrected. The Methods says Hochberg's procedure with k=2 is applied, but the Results never state whether the reported p-values survive this correction.
   - Location: Methods statistical overview; Results Q3 first paragraph
   - Proposed fix: Add a parenthetical in Results Q3: "Species richness positively predicted coral condition (standardized β = 0.27, t₇₉ = 2.42, p = 0.018, p_Hochberg = 0.036; Fig. 5A)" or, if the p-values reported are already Hochberg-corrected, state that explicitly: "(p = 0.018, Hochberg-corrected for two a priori tests)."
   - Ripple risk: Results Q3 first paragraph only.
